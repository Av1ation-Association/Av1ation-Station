## Before you start

The video needs a queued source. **Decoded** additionally needs a resolvable decode graph, and **Encoded** needs a detected scene with a retained chunk at the playhead.

## Open the source control

Open the Scenes tab and find the source button in the player controls. It names the current source, for example **Preview source: Original**. Selecting it opens the three choices and, when Encoded is active, the source it should decode from.

## Compare Original and Decoded

<Steps>
<Step title="Pause on a frame" result="The playhead sits on a frame you want to compare.">
Use the timeline or the frame field. The same frame is the comparison point for every source.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose Original" result="The player shows the queued source file.">
Original reads the media the run decodes. It is the reference for what the filters and encoder start from.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose Decoded" result="The player shows the decoded graph at the same scene-relative frame.">
Decoded reads the filter graph, so a crop, resize, or denoise is visible. If the graph cannot resolve, the choice explains the prerequisite instead of showing a black frame.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Compare Encoded

Choose Encoded while the playhead is inside a scene with a retained chunk. The player switches to that chunk and aligns its stream start to the scene. If the playhead has no scene, the choice is disabled and names the reason.

## Switch without losing position

Changing source keeps the scene-relative frame, not the absolute timecode of the previous stream. That is what makes a comparison meaningful: an encoded chunk starts at its scene's first frame, so the player maps the playhead into the chunk instead of seeking to the source timestamp.

<Callout tone="tip" title="Frame, not wall clock">
Read the frame field when comparing. A timecode from a variable-frame-rate source can round differently across the three streams.
</Callout>

## Understand unavailable sources

- **Decoded disabled**: the input has no resolvable decode graph, or a required provider is missing.
- **Encoded disabled**: there is no scene at the playhead, or the scene has no retained chunk.
- **Encoded from unavailable**: the chunk was retained but its source no longer resolves.
- **Paired browser**: the media token or host path may not be available; the LAN browser explains the limitation rather than falling back silently.

## Check your result

The source control reports the selected source, and the frame field stays on the same frame across switches. Returning to Original should show the source frame you started from.
